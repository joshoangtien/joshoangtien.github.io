# Home

Mode: Operate
Audience: developers looking up a utility fast

## Direction contract

THESIS: Search is the product. A glass bento of tools, not a sidebar dashboard of identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night glass on #07080c, ice highlight, Bricolage Grotesque display, Be Vietnam Pro UI. Tiles are liquid glass with 3D tilt. Light mode is warm paper with milk glass.

STORY: Arrive, search or pick a category, open a workbench. Author is not the hero.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header with TOOLS + search pill + theme. Hero “Find a tool.” and a large glass search. Below: one large favorite tile and category glass tiles. No sidebar.

FORM: Search-first glass bento. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
